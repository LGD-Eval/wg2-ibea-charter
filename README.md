# FG-TIDA WG2: Identity, Behaviour and Enforcement for AI Agents (IBEA)

Charter of FG-TIDA Working Group 2 (provisional, subject to approval at the first FG-TIDA meeting, Paris, 30 November to 1 December 2026). The working name IBEA is provisional.

**Co-chairs:** Artur Hecker (Huawei Technologies), Cyril Gorlla (CTGT).

The working group addresses the mismatch between an AI agent's identity, its behaviour and the enforcement of the authority it acts under. Its central object is one action, checked when it is taken against a pinned reference, producing a verdict a relying party can recompute. It joins FG-TIDA/themes#4 and FG-TIDA/themes#6 (FG-TIDA/themes#36).
